Activities for family conversations

Three complete ideas to explore saving and everyday choices, with no signup or purchase.

Talking about money with kids ages 4–8

Start with one small everyday choice. You do not need to explain every part of your family finances: listen to one question and think it through together.

Saving activity: one container, one goal and one drawing

Make one small goal visible and notice progress together. Try this without buying the book, printing anything or using real money.

Saving, waiting and helping: questions for family reading

Use these questions with any story about everyday choices. If you do not have a book handy, try an imaginary situation and listen to your child’s ideas.
