How we use your information

When you request a free resource, we ask for the grown-up’s name and email to record the request and deliver the material. We do not need your child’s information.

Resource delivery

The service uses Cloudflare to store the request and file, and Resend to send the email. We record whether the provider accepts the message and when a download is requested. The download link expires after seven days.

Requesting a resource does not automatically subscribe you to a newsletter.

Website measurement

We use Google Analytics to understand which pages and actions help families. Custom events do not include the name or email you enter in the form. Your browser can limit this measurement.

Amazon

Purchase links take you to Amazon. Purchases, delivery and associated information are handled there under Amazon’s own policies.

Contact and information requests

For questions or requests to access, correct or delete the information you provided, email contac@ekwonderkids.com.
